Tips & shortcuts

Everything Easy Note can do, on one page. Nothing here is required — you can get a long way with double-click and type.

The basics

  • Double-click any empty space to make a note, then just type.
  • Drag a note by its top bar to move it; drag the bottom-right corner to resize.
  • Hover a note to reveal its buttons — they stay hidden so the board looks clean.
  • Paste anything. Text arrives clean, without fonts and colours from the page you copied it from. Screenshots paste straight in.

Moving around

  • Scroll to move around the board, or hold Space and drag it.
  • ⌘ / Ctrl + scroll, or pinch on a trackpad, to zoom. Zooming follows your cursor.
  • Lost a note? Click Fit in the bottom-right — everything on the page comes back into view.

Tidying up

  • Drag a box across empty space to select several notes, or hold Shift and click them.
  • Drag any selected note and the whole group moves together.
  • The toolbar that appears will line them up, space them evenly, or snap them into a grid.
  • Lock a note with the padlock and it can no longer be deleted — including by a bulk delete. It still moves and edits normally.

Pages

  • Double-click a page name to rename it. Enter saves, Esc cancels.
  • Drag a page up or down to reorder it. Drop it on another page to nest it inside.
  • Drag notes onto a page in the list to move them there — select several first and they all go together.
  • Drag the sidebar's edge to resize it; double-click that edge to reset the width.
  • Each page remembers its own position and zoom.

Finding things

  • ⌘F searches every page at once. Before you type, it lists the notes you edited most recently.
  • Pick a result and Easy Note opens that page and zooms straight to the note.

Sharing your screen

Click 🙈 in the bottom-right, or press Alt + B, and every note goes blurred — along with search results, which would otherwise show the same text. Page names stay readable so you can still find your way around.

  • The setting sticks, so notes are already blurred the next time you open a tab. They never flash readable first.
  • Hovering a note does not reveal it — a stray cursor cannot uncover anything.
  • Click into a note and that one note becomes readable while you type, so you can still work with the blur on.

Syncing

Click the cloud in the bottom-right and sign in with Google, and your notes follow you to every computer you use Chrome on. They go into a private folder that Easy Note cannot see out of — it has no access to anything else in your Drive.

Sync checks every couple of minutes, so give it a moment after editing elsewhere, or press Sync now.
