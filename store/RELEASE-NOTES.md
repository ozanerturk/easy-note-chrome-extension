Easy Note v3.0.0

Your new tab is a notes canvas. v3 is a ground-up rewrite that keeps the
two-second capture and adds room to organise.

Google Drive sync
  Sign in with Google and notes, pages and pasted images follow you
  between machines. Data lives in a hidden per-user app folder, so it
  never appears in My Drive and the extension cannot see any other file.
  Merging is per note rather than per board, so two machines editing
  different notes both keep their work, and deletes propagate instead of
  the note reappearing.

Infinite canvas
  Scroll to pan, Ctrl/Cmd-scroll or pinch to zoom about the cursor, and
  Fit to bring every note back on screen.

Pages
  A sidebar tree, nested as deep as you like. Drag notes onto a page to
  move them; the whole selection travels. Each page keeps its own pan
  and zoom.

Lock notes
  Guards a note from deletion while leaving it movable and editable.

Also: multi-select with align, distribute and grid; search across every
page that lists recent notes before you type; paste text and images;
per-note colours, fullscreen and last-edited times.

Upgrading
  Notes from the published v1 are imported automatically on first run,
  keeping headings, lists, links, bold, italic, underline, strikethrough
  and note colours. v1's database is opened read-only and never
  modified, so rolling back loses nothing.

Known limits
  Sync compares wall-clock timestamps, so a badly wrong clock can win a
  conflict it should not. Two devices syncing within the same few
  seconds can drop one update. Sync runs every couple of minutes and on
  demand — it is for one person across several machines, not live
  collaboration.
